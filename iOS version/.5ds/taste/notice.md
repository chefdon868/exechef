# Lazy defaults

These are the looks AI design falls into when nobody decides. This list is an end check, not a ban: each is fine **only with a reason from the concept**, written in `.5ds/taste/art-direction.md`. A cream page or a dark page made with care is fine; the great sites use both. What fails is reaching for one because it is the likely answer. If the owner's own words ask for one, the owner wins. Avoiding this list is not the goal either: a page can miss every item and still be plain. The goal is the bar you set in step 2 of `.5ds/taste/instructions.md`.

What is lazy is the default, not the element. Pill buttons and badges, 1 radius kept on every card, a word that carries the meaning in colour or italic, and a faint grain are Omar's craft (`.5ds/taste/craft.md`) when done with care. They are never on this list.

## The first wave
- Purple or blue gradients, gradient text.
- Inter, Roboto or the system font everywhere.
- The stock hero, reached for because it is what comes out: a badge, a headline, 2 buttons and a screenshot, centred, saying nothing about this product.
- 3 identical cards with an icon on top.
- Emoji as icons.
- Glass effects on everything; a grey drop shadow on every box.

## The second wave
- A cream or beige ground with a high contrast serif and a terracotta or rust accent.
- Near black with 1 acid or neon accent.
- A dark ground (night blue, bottle green, near black) with a gold, amber or yellow action, sold as a lamp, a glow, a shopfront, signwriting or a vintage screen. The commonest dark look of all.
- Newspaper hairlines with square corners everywhere.
- Tracked all-caps eyebrow labels and kicker labels above headings.
- Monospace for small labels, as a "technical" costume.
- 01 / 02 / 03 numbering on content that is not a sequence.
- 1 word in colour or italic as decoration, saying nothing the others do not.
- A hero row of big numbers with small labels.
- Coloured left or top borders on cards; a hairline border plus a wide soft shadow.
- Heavy grain or paper texture as costume; hand drawn mascots.

## Copy
- Headlines made of 2 short aphorisms; buzzwords; long dashes everywhere.
- Themed rewrites of standard labels ("Remember this traveller" for "Remember me").
- Invented numbers, logos and testimonials.

## Process
- Light or dark chosen by the category, hue chosen by the category (blue for software, green for health).
- The same fade and slide on every section; a hover lift on every card.
- Decoration standing in for an idea.
- Costume: the subject's things drawn literally (a roofline for a name, bricks for a city, a chalkboard for a cafe) in place of an idea.
- Timid on a showcase page: 1 fade on everything, small type, every section the same pace, nothing to remember.
- A mood metaphor (glow, lamp, warmth, calm, light in the dark) as the governing idea, when it says nothing about this product.
- 3 directions that are really 1: the same ground lightness, or action colours from the same family.
