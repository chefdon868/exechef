# Where to look

A vetted list of places to look: to study craft, spacing and how others solved a similar problem, never to copy a look. Every entry has its exact address, what it adds, and the date it was checked. Pick from this list or ask the owner; never send the owner to a site that is not here without saying it is new to you. The bar (step 2 of the art direction) comes from the best real work in the field; the idea comes from the audience's own world.

Before a new screen or flow, look at 3 to 5 real examples of the same thing, note what makes each good (spacing, type sizes, colour, the 1 thing it does best), then design. Take the system, never the pixels: no other brand's words, logo or pictures. A reference the owner names means only the part they name.
