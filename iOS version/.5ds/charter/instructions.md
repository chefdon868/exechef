# How to use the Charter

- `charter.md` is the owner's. Read it at the start of a new chat.
- If it is still empty, fill it with the owner, 1 question at a time. Ask what the project is for. Write down their words, with the date.
- Then ask, 1 at a time, what you may do without asking, what you should ask first about, and what you must never do. Keep each list short.
- Only change `charter.md` when the owner tells you to, and write what they said.
